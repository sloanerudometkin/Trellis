The three directions

Each card below shows the icon on its own, how it sits next to the wordmark, and how it holds up shrunk down to app-icon size — that tiny size is where most people will actually meet your logo first, as a browser tab or home-screen icon, so it has to survive getting small.

Concept A

The Waypath

Pathero

A single confident route through a few turns, ending in one solid point. The open circle at the start is you, right now. Each bend is a decision Pathero already prioritized for you. The solid dot at the top is today's most important move — the "what to do next."

Best for: app icon, favicon, tab 16px

Concept B

The Waypoint P

Pathero

A single letter carries the whole brand — until you notice the arrow built into it. The counter of the "P" is cut as a forward-pointing wedge instead of a plain hole, so even the negative space is pointing somewhere. Reads as the most "established software company" of the three.

Best for: brand recognition, merch 16px

Concept C

The Convergence

Pathero

Three separate points — paid search, SEO, AEO — pulled into one line, one plan. It's the most literal illustration of "one coordinated strategy, all in one place," and tells that story before anyone reads a word of copy.

Best for: pitch decks, site hero 16px

If you want one recommendation: Concept A, the Waypath.

It holds up best at tiny sizes, which matters more than it seems like it should — most people will meet this logo first as a small tab icon, not a big hero image. It's also the most literal read of the brand promise: shows you where you are, what matters, and what to do next.

If you'd rather lead with the "three channels, one plan" story up front, Concept C is a strong second choice, and it can still live alongside the Waypath as a companion mark for decks and the marketing site.
